🍫 巧克力熔岩蛋糕

Chocolate Fondant

⭐ 9/10 · ⭐ Sal Approved · 份量：3–4 個 / Makes 3–4

純素巧克力熔岩蛋糕，切開會有流心的巧克力。

Vegan chocolate fondant with a gooey chocolate center.

食材 · Ingredients

  • 香蕉泥 50 g · Mashed banana 50 g
  • 純素奶油 40 g · Vegan butter 40 g
  • 豆奶 35 g · Soy milk 35 g
  • 砂糖 60 g · Sugar 60 g
  • 中筋麵粉 35 g · All-purpose flour 35 g
  • 玉米澱粉 10 g · Cornstarch 10 g
  • 可可粉 20 g · Cocoa powder 20 g
  • 杏仁粉 10 g · Almond flour 10 g
  • 泡打粉 1/4 tsp · Baking powder 1/4 tsp
  • 鹽一小撮 · A pinch of salt

做法 · Steps

  1. 純素奶油融化，稍微放涼。
    Melt the vegan butter and let it cool slightly.
  2. 加香蕉泥、豆奶、砂糖，攪拌均勻。
    Add the mashed banana, soy milk, and sugar; mix until combined.
  3. 加入麵粉、玉米澱粉、可可粉、杏仁粉、泡打粉、鹽。
    Add the flour, cornstarch, cocoa powder, almond flour, baking powder, and salt.
  4. 拌到剛好沒有乾粉就停。
    Mix just until no dry flour remains — don't overmix.
  5. 杯子蛋糕模具抹奶油，分成 3 份。
    Grease the cupcake molds with butter and divide the batter into 3 portions.
  6. 200°C 烤 9–11 分鐘。
    Bake at 200°C for 9–11 minutes.
  7. 出爐後等 1–2 分鐘再脫模。
    Let sit for 1–2 minutes after baking before unmolding.
